Flash or Pro? We priced both on 120,000 cold emails.

Pro costs about 5x Flash per token. Everyone assumes it converts enough better to pay for itself. We ran 17 A/B tests across both tiers, on real campaigns, and priced every reply and every click.

01 The answer

Each tier wins one match.

A positive reply costs $193 on the Pro tier and $1,424 on Flash, $122 on our best Pro workflow. A website visit costs $2 on Flash and $4 on Pro, $1 on our best Flash workflow.

02 The twist
36 of 36
positive replies came from an email with no link in it

Put a link in the email and people click it instead of writing back. Leave the link out and they reply. Flash or Pro does not change that. It only changes what each reply or click costs you.
03 Who writes back

Owners reply. The C-suite clicks.

Owners write back the most, 8.1 positive replies per 10,000 Pro emails, against 1.1 for the C-suite. Companies of one to ten people reply the most, 6.3 per 10,000. The click goes the other way: a C-suite reader visits for $1 on Flash, a founder for $8, and a company over 1,000 people for $1 against $7 for one under ten.

04 Where and when

Health practices write back. Monday carries the reply.

Health and wellness practices reply the most, 8.7 positive replies per 10,000 Pro emails, medical practices 4.4, hospitals 2.2. Monday is the best day to send, 6.3 per 10,000, and Friday the worst at 1.8.
