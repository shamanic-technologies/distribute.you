Flash or Pro? We priced both on 125,000 cold emails.

Pro costs about 5x Flash per token. Everyone assumes it converts enough better to pay for itself. We ran 25 A/B tests across both tiers, on real campaigns for 34 clients, and priced every reply and every click.

THE ANSWER
A positive reply costs $198 on the Pro tier and $1,461 on Flash, $139 on our best Pro workflow. A website visit costs $2 on Flash and $6 on Pro, $1 on our best Flash workflow.
Each tier wins one match.

THE TWIST
4.9 positive replies per 10,000 Pro emails with no link, against 1.5 with one. Put a link in the email and people click it instead of writing back. Leave the link out and they reply. Flash or Pro does not change that. It only changes what each reply or click costs you.

WHO WRITES BACK
Owners write back the most, 7.6 positive replies per 10,000 Pro emails, against 1 for the C-suite. Companies of one to ten people reply the most, 6.2 per 10,000. The click goes the other way: a director visits for $1 on Flash and a founder for $7, and a company over 1,000 people visits for $1 against $7 for one under ten.

WHERE AND WHEN
Management consulting replies the most, 11.5 positive replies per 10,000 Pro emails, financial services 10.4, health and wellness 7.9. Monday is the best weekday to send, 6.1 per 10,000, and Thursday the worst at 1.8.

Optimising for website visits? Use the Flash tier: a short email, a link, one to two paragraphs. $1 a visit on our best workflow.
Optimising for replies? Use the Pro tier: no link, room to write, sent early in the week. $139 a positive reply on our best workflow.
