The question

Ask what cold email costs and you usually get a reply rate, or a price per lead. Neither tells you what you pay for the thing you want: a prospect who answers with interest.

We run cold email for our clients and bill each campaign for what it spends. So we can answer from the bill. We added up what our clients paid for the emails that ask for a reply, and divided by the positive replies those emails brought back.

How to read your own number

Set the price of a positive reply against what one new customer is worth to you. A positive reply is the start of a conversation, not a sale, so count how many of them it takes you to sign one customer.

Our average spans every client and every offer we ran. Yours will sit above or below it, depending on what you sell and to whom. The point is to see it: a price you measure on your own campaigns, not one you are quoted up front.
