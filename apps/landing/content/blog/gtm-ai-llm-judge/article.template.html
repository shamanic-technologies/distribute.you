What Jev is

Jev 1.13 is a model by TypeSafe. You ask a yes or no question, a choice among options, or a level on a scale. Jev returns a probability for every answer. A writing model such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or Gemini 3 gives one answer with the same confidence whether it is sure or guessing. Jev tells you when it is guessing.

Jev bills input tokens only; the answer is free. Across our pipes, 1,000 judgments come to {{raw volume.perThousand}} as billed.

1. Screen the cold filtered list from Apollo before you pay

We build each campaign's list in Apollo with cold filters: titles, industries, company sizes. Apollo shows each person's name, title and company for free. The email address costs a credit. Filters are blunt, so before any credit is spent, Jev reads every person against the buyer the client described: "Does this candidate belong to the target audience the client described?"

You pay for about 1 lead in {{raw screen.keptOneIn}}: the one who fits. Jev turns the others away before the paid reveal. One client sells shockwave devices to chiropractors. The filters also returned hospitalists and a urology physician: Jev turned each of them away. A "Chiropractic Physician / Owner" was a clear fit and was revealed.

A screen costs a fraction of a cent, a reveal about {{raw price.reveal}}. Each verdict is stored with the person and the exact audience text, so nobody is judged twice. When a client rewrites the audience, everyone is judged again.

2. Tell people from machines in replies

{{chart replies}}

1 reply in {{raw machine.oneIn}} to our cold emails is a robot: out-of-office messages and auto-replies, in every language ("Ich geniesse gerade Ferien"). We filter them out, so every reply you see is from a real person. Our reply classifier, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, labels each reply and Jev checks the label. In the inbox, Jev decides on its own: a sender is hidden from your contacts as automated only when Jev is at least 75% sure.

3. When Jev is unsure, a person answers

A clinic CEO wrote "Unfortunately I can't till next week." Out of office, or a meeting request? Jev said it was not sure. So a person answered that reply, and a warm lead stayed warm.

Jev is sure most of the time, so a person only steps in where it counts. On the Apollo list we keep anyone Jev finds more likely to fit than not, so no real buyer is lost; before hiding a sender from your contacts, Jev must be very sure.
