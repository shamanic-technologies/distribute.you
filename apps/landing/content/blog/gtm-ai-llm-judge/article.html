What Jev is

Jev 1.13 is a model by TypeSafe. You ask a yes or no question, a choice among options, or a level on a scale. Jev returns a probability for every answer. A writing model such as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash or Gemini 3 gives one answer with the same confidence whether it is sure or guessing. Jev tells you when it is guessing.

Jev bills input tokens only; the answer is free. Across our pipes, 1,000 judgments come to $0.09 as billed.

1. Only leads matching your ICP get your emails

We build each campaign's list in Apollo with cold filters: titles, industries, company sizes. Apollo shows each person's name, title and company for free. The email address costs a credit. Filters are blunt, so before any credit is spent, Jev reads every person against the client's ICP, written in their own words: "Does this candidate belong to the target audience the client described?"

Your budget goes to your ICP. Jev clears every person before the paid reveal, so the emails we write reach the people who match it. One client sells shockwave devices to chiropractors. The filters also returned hospitalists and a urology physician: Jev turned each of them away. A "Chiropractic Physician / Owner" was a clear fit and was revealed.

A screen costs a fraction of a cent, a reveal about $0.10. Each verdict is stored with the person and the exact audience text, so nobody is judged twice. When a client rewrites the audience, everyone is judged again.

2. Every reply you read is a real person

Every reply you read is a real person. Out-of-office messages and auto-replies, in every language ("Ich geniesse gerade Ferien"), are filtered out before they reach you, and your numbers count people only. Our reply classifier, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, labels each reply and Jev checks the label. In the inbox, Jev decides on its own: a sender is hidden from your contacts as automated only when Jev is at least 75% sure.
