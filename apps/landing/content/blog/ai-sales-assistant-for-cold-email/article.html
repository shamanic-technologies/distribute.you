Most founders we talk to do not want another sales tool. They want someone who does the prospecting so they can keep building, and who only interrupts them when a buyer answers. That is what an AI sales assistant for cold email should be: not a writing helper, but the person running your outbound.

The job, in one line

Find the people who buy what you sell, write to them, learn which emails get answered, and hand you the ones who want to talk. Everything else is plumbing, and plumbing is the part a founder should never see.

Five things to check before you pick one

  1. It starts from your website. You paste a URL, it reads what you sell and who buys it, and you correct what is wrong. If setup is a form with forty fields, you are the assistant.
  2. It builds the list itself. Finding the right people is most of the work. An assistant that expects you to upload a CSV has skipped it.
  3. It tests, not just writes. One good email is luck. Several versions per audience, and the one that gets answered kept, is a method.
  4. It follows up. A first email that gets no answer is not a no. The assistant sends the follow-ups and stops the moment someone answers.
  5. It shows you the cost. You should see what each interested reply cost you, per audience, without asking.

It should reach you where you already are

A founder does not want a new app to check. The interested replies should land in the inbox you already use, with the whole thread, so you answer them like any other message. The dashboard is there when you want the numbers, and optional when you do not.
