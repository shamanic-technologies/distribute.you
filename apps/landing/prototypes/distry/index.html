What clients are saying

Common questions

What do I need to get started?

Just your website. Drop the URL in, our agent works out what you sell and who buys it, and shows you the plan to confirm. Then you add your first $10, and your campaign starts right away.

What is the price?

Pricing is usage based. Each action is billed at the vendor's API price with 0% markup, from a prepaid balance. Every price is public on the catalog, and your dashboard shows what each reply and meeting came to.

Is it really 0% markup?

Yes. Every account opened since October 29, 2026 pays the vendor's public API price for each action. The live list is on the catalog, and the same list is public as JSON.

Can I run it on my own server?

Yes. The GTM Factory is open source under the MIT license. Run it with your own API keys, and your data stays in your own database.

Whose mailboxes send the emails?

Everything goes out from mailboxes and domains we own and warm. Your mailbox never touches cold outreach, and there is nothing to set up on your side. Replies land with us first, we qualify them, and the interested ones are forwarded to your inbox.

What is the ROI you show?

Revenue attributed to the campaign, divided by what you paid for it. It is measured, not projected: it starts at zero and climbs as meetings and signups turn into paying customers. You see it per offer, per channel and per audience.

How much control do I have?

You confirm the offer, the audiences and the emails before anything leaves, and you can pause everything in one click. Your balance is the cap, and auto reload tops it up at the level you choose. Most clients look once a week.

Can I bring my own leads?

Yes. Upload a CSV and we run it as its own audience, with its own price per outcome, next to the ones we find.

Do you guarantee results?

We guarantee the measurement: what every outcome came to is always visible, and the budget is always yours to stop. Want a team accountable for the result? The GTM Agency runs it for you.
