What clients are saying

Common questions

What do I need to get started?

Your AI and your website. Add our MCP server, tell your AI what you sell, and Distry shows you the plan to confirm. Your campaign goes out right away.

What is the price?

Free with your own keys or your Claude subscription. The $99/month plan runs on our keys at 0% markup, with a 3-day free trial. See both plans.

Which AI can I use?

Conductor, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Hermes, Grok Bot and Dots, through our MCP server or the API. WhatsApp, Telegram and email work too. Pick yours.

Which keys do I need on the free plan?

Only the ones your campaign uses. When a campaign launches, Distry lists the keys its plan needs and asks for each one through your AI. Every AI step can run on your own Claude subscription.

Whose mailboxes send the emails?

Everything goes out from mailboxes and domains we own and warm. Your mailbox never touches cold outreach, and there is nothing to set up on your side. Replies land with us first, we qualify them, and the interested ones are forwarded to your inbox.

What is the ROI you show?

Revenue attributed to the campaign, divided by what you paid for it. It is measured, not projected: it starts at zero and climbs as meetings and signups turn into paying customers. You see it per offer, per channel and per audience.

How much control do I have?

You confirm the offer, the audiences and the emails before anything leaves, and you can pause everything in one click. Most clients look once a week.

Can I bring my own leads?

Yes. Upload a CSV and we run it as its own audience, with its own price per outcome, next to the ones we find.

What happens when my credit runs out?

Your campaigns pause. Your AI, WhatsApp and email answer with a link to add $100 a month, and everything starts again once it is in.
