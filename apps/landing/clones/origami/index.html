Frequently asked questions.

What is Origami?

Origami gets customers for you. Enter your website, and it finds the best ways to reach the people most likely to buy, from people already warm to you to whole new markets, then runs the campaigns for you.

What is a play?

A play is one way of getting customers: a group of people with a reason to hear from you, and the outreach that goes with it. Origami picks the best plays for your business.

Do I need to know what to run?

No. Enter your website and Origami works out the plays. You can also describe what you do, or give it your own idea.

Can I check messages before they go out?

Yes, if you want to. Turn on approval and every message waits for you. Otherwise Origami sends for you.

What do I need to connect?

Nothing to start. To send, connect your email or let Origami set up sending inboxes for you. You can also connect your CRM, such as Salesforce, HubSpot or Attio.

How does pricing work?

Plans start at $99 a month, enough for over 1,000 researched and verified leads. There are no per-seat fees, and your whole team is included.
