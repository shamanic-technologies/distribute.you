How can Revid.ai transform my content creation game?
Revid turns a prompt, script, link, product page, audio file, or PDF into an editable video workflow. It can draft the script, generate or source visuals, add voice and captions, and prepare the result for social publishing.

Yes, 100%! You own all the content you create with Revid.ai, forever. Every video, script, and asset generated through our platform belongs entirely to you. There are no hidden clauses or shared ownership - your creative work is yours to use, modify, and monetize as you see fit.

Absolutely! Revid.ai is designed with creators like you in mind. Our intuitive interface and AI-driven tools make it a breeze to produce professional-grade videos, even if you've never edited before. The possibilities are endless! Whether you want to repurpose a blog post, turn a podcast into a video, create a viral TikTok from scratch, or produce engaging product demos and explainer videos, Revid.ai has you covered. Our AI can generate scripts from any text or URL, find the perfect viral hooks, and even create videos automatically from your favorite content sources. If you can dream it, Revid.ai can help you create it.

While our AI is incredibly powerful, you always remain in the driver's seat. Revid.ai provides a foundation of high-quality, engaging content that you can then customize to your heart's content. From adding a professional sounding voice-over and branding elements to fine-tuning the visuals and pacing, our platform empowers you to create videos that are authentically yours.
How can Revid.ai help me grow my audience and business?
Revid helps you test more hooks, formats, stories, and product angles without rebuilding every video from scratch. You can edit each result, publish it to your channels, and use Auto-Mode to keep a repeatable content plan moving. Audience results still depend on the idea, offer, and distribution—no tool can guarantee views.

Revid combines generation, editing, captions, voice, publishing, automation, API, MCP, and CLI access in one product. Instead of stopping at a generated clip, it gives you an editable project you can adapt for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, product marketing, or automated workflows.
