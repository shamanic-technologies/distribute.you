Information collected through your use of the Services

The Services include our autonomous personal assistant that acts on your behalf, should you choose to engage our personal assistant. The personal assistant thinks, plans, and acts to help you complete your everyday tasks.

To effectively perform its duties, the personal assistant is designed to be available when engaged and, depending on the permissions you grant, may have access to information you share with and make available to it. This may include, for example, information from applications and services you have connected, text and documents that you transmit, and other text or data that is made available to us through your or connected integrations and accounts. This may include the content of messages, emails, or other private communications or materials that you choose to make accessible to the personal assistant. The Services may also collect audio and voice data, should you choose to enable these features.

Depending on the permissions you grant and how you interact with the personal assistant, it may have access to information that is considered sensitive. For example, you may provide your payment information to the personal assistant so it can book rides on your behalf or your username and password for third-party accounts so that the personal assistant can sign into these accounts on your behalf. Or, for instance, you may use the personal assistant to process health-related information, for example, when the personal assistant books a medical appointment for you or summarizes an email received from your healthcare provider. As noted above, should you choose to provide the personal assistant with access, it will also have access to the content of your messages, emails, and other private communications that you have made available to us through connected integrations.

As described above, we use certain information collected through your use of the Services, including as described in this section, to develop and improve our products and technologies, including to evaluate, fine-tune, and train the AI models that power our products and technology. If you do not want us to use your information to train AI models, you can opt out by visiting app.instinct.com/settings. Even if you opt out, we may still use your information for AI model training when that information is flagged for safety review to improve our ability to detect harmful content, enforce our policies, or advance our safety research. This opt out applies on a go-forward basis and applies only to model training and not, for clarity, other types of improvements to the Services. Please note that such opt out may limit the ability of our Services to better address your specific use case. We may still use AI models previously trained, fine-tuned or improved on your information prior to your opting out. We do not use information received directly from Google Workspace to evaluate, fine-tune, train, or improve AI models (as detailed in the "Information Collected From Google Workspace" section below).

Please note that the Services and the personal assistant are not a professional advisor, so you should not rely on it for actions requiring financial, legal, medical, or other advice or judgment.

Information collected from Google Workspace

If you choose to link your Google Workspace to the Services, we receive access to your Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides and Google Tasks, and also receive related file metadata, message metadata, calendar event details, and other content that Google shares with us about your account and use of Google's products and services via Google Workspace APIs. We use information collected from Google Workspace to provide the Services, including so that the personal assistant can take actions on your behalf, and to personalize your use of the Services.

Our use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use information received from Google Workspace APIs to evaluate, fine-tune, train, or improve AI models, or for serving ads, including retargeting, personalized or interested-based advertising. To the extent that third parties process information collected from Google Workspace on our behalf (including third-party AI model providers), we only permit them to process this information on our behalf and to help us provide the Services (and for certain legally permissible purposes such as maintaining security and complying with law). In particular, we do not disclose raw or derived Workspace or Photos API user data to third party AI services, including third-party AI model providers, for model training or secondary purposes.

At any time, you may revoke our access to your Google Workspace and request deletion of information collected via the Google Workspace API. You may revoke access by initiating a conversation with the Instinct agent, or disconnecting via your settings within Instinct's Workspace. You may also delete the information we previously collected from Google Workspace by deleting your account within Instinct's Workspace.
