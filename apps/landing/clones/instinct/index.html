Instinct is a personal assistant that understands what you're working on and what's important to you.

The interface is simple: there are no new interfaces. You can text or call it. It can use a phone and computer in the same way that humans do.

It can help you dispute an unwarranted bill, send birthday gifts to friends, order groceries, schedule doctor's appointments, or plan a weekend getaway.
