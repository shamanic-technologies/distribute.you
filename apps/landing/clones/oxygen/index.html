FAQs

Read the documentation ↗
Who is OXYGEN for?

The GTM teams of sales-led B2B companies: sales, marketing and RevOps working a defined list of target accounts, usually with a few sellers and deals large enough that every account matters. AI agents research the accounts, find the right people and run the outreach alongside your team.

How is OXYGEN different from building it ourselves with Clay, Apollo and a sequencer?

You get the same building blocks already connected in one workspace: company and people search, enrichment, email and LinkedIn sequences, a CRM and workflows. There is nothing to wire together or keep running, every result shows where it came from and what it cost, and your team works in one place instead of five tools.

Does anything get sent or spent without our approval?

By default, yes, in your chat with an Oxygent, OXYGEN's in-app AI agent. The chat starts on Auto-approve, so sends, sequence starts and paid steps run without an approval card, each within its own credit cap and your balance. Switch the chat to Ask for approval, and sends, sequence starts and larger paid steps wait for you. Every run is logged with what it did, what it cost and where each value came from.

How good is the contact data?

OXYGEN runs a waterfall across several email and phone providers, checks each email’s deliverability and records which provider found every value. You pay only when a value is found, so you can test it on a sample of your own accounts with the free credits before you commit.

How much does a credit cost and how does it get billed?

One dollar buys 100 credits, and the $499, $999 and $1,999 plans add bonus credits on top: 52,395, 109,890 and 229,885 a month. Credits pay for managed data, AI calls on OXYGEN's keys, and the sending accounts listed in the pricing table. Calls on your own provider key carry a 0.05-credit ($0.0005) platform fee, and each workflow step that calls a tool costs the same. Extra credits cost $1.30 per 100 and never expire. Yearly plans save about 17% with the full year charged upfront. Credits arrive monthly.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. A new workspace starts on the free tier with a one-time grant of 250 credits, no card and no time limit. Paid plans start at $49 a month, and if you cancel later your workspace stays on the free tier with everything you built.

Do I need to use Claude Code or Codex?

You can, but you don’t need to. OXYGEN has a built-in Workspace Agent and a full web app, so you can run your whole motion without a coding agent. If you already work in Claude Code or Codex, connect it through the MCP server or CLI and it operates the same workspace.

Do I need my own email infrastructure?

No. OXYGEN provides managed Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 inboxes, Azure tenants and sending domains, billed in credits, or you can connect the mailboxes you already own. Every account and inbox price is listed in credits and dollars in the pricing table.

Does it work with our CRM and the tools we already use?

Yes. HubSpot syncs both ways, Attio receives updates and Pipedrive can be imported; there is no Salesforce sync. Leads can also go into your Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist or HeyReach campaigns, and the integration catalog lists everything else you can connect.

What is a GTM agent platform?

A GTM agent platform is software where AI agents do go-to-market work, like account research, outreach and CRM updates, next to your team. OXYGEN is one: an agentic GTM platform built for B2B GTM teams. You give an agent a job in plain words, and it works in the same CRM, lead lists and sequences as your team. Your data, approvals and costs stay in one workspace, so you can see what each agent did and what it spent.

Is there a CLI for GTM and CRM work?

Yes, OXYGEN has a CLI, a command-line tool that runs your GTM and CRM work from the terminal. You can search and update CRM records, build and enrich lead lists, and run sequences in the same workspace as the web app. Previewing a search is free, paid runs need your approval and a credit limit, and sequences start only when you approve them. The CLI guide shows how to install it and sign in.
