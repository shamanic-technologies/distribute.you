FAQs

Read the documentation ↗
How much does a credit cost and how does it get billed?

One dollar buys 100 credits, and every plan’s monthly credits are priced at that face value. Credits are only drawn when Oxygen spends on your behalf: managed data or AI providers running on our keys, and the sending accounts you connect, which carry the monthly amounts listed in the pricing table. Bring your own provider keys and those calls draw no credits. Extra credits cost $1.25 per 100 and never expire.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. A new workspace starts on the free tier with a one-time grant of 1,000 credits, no card and no time limit. Paid plans start at $49 a month, and if you cancel later your workspace stays on the free tier with everything you built.

Do I need to use Claude Code or Codex?

You can, but you don’t need to. Oxygen has a built-in Workspace Agent and a full web app, so you can run your whole motion without a coding agent. If you already work in Claude Code or Codex, connect it through the MCP server or CLI and it operates the same workspace.

Do I need my own email infrastructure?

No. Oxygen provides managed inboxes and sending domains, billed in credits, or you can connect the mailboxes you already own. Every seat and inbox cost is listed in the pricing table.

Can I keep the tools I already use?

Yes. Start with one workflow and connect the tools you already run. The integration catalog lists the providers and actions your motion needs, and results can sync back to your CRM.

Can I run separate workspaces for multiple clients?

Yes. Use separate organizations and workspaces to keep each client’s data, context and workflows distinct. Your team can switch between the workspaces they have access to.
